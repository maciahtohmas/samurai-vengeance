How to become champion

  • Sign in with a free account and play. Your best stage of the week is recorded on its own.
  • Open the leaderboard and pick This Week to see where you stand. The week resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC.
  • The champion gets a crown on the leaderboard until the next one is crowned, the Champion sword style for good, a place on this page, and a shoutout on our TikTok.

Scores are checked before a champion is crowned. Full rules are available on request at contact@samuraivengeance.com.
