Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Samurai Vengeance is operated by Billfiti Studios. This policy explains what the game stores, why, and how to get it back or get rid of it.

What we collect

If you play as a guest: nothing leaves your device. Progress is kept in your browser's local storage and we never see it.

If you create an account: email address, display name, gameplay statistics (stage reached, kills, playtime, coins earned, combo scores) and device type.

We do not collect or store payment card information at any point. See Payments below.

How we use it

  • To save your progress and sync it across devices
  • To show your rank on the global leaderboard
  • To personalise daily challenges

We do not sell your data to third parties, ever.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout pages. Your card details are entered on Stripe's domain and are never transmitted to or stored by us. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, along with the email address you gave Stripe, so the purchase can be attached to your account.

Advertising

The game may display advertising to players without a Premium purchase. Advertising partners may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure and serve ads. Premium removes advertising entirely.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your consent choice and your local game progress. We set no first-party tracking cookies of our own.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You may request an export of your data, correction of anything inaccurate, or complete deletion of your account and everything attached to it, at any time. Both export and deletion are available in the game under Profile → Delete Account, or by email.

Data retention

Account data is retained while the account is active. On deletion, all associated database records are permanently removed within 30 days.

Children

Accounts are not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU and EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children below those ages. The game is playable as a guest with no data collection at all.

Contact

Questions, export requests or deletion requests: contact@samuraivengeance.com
