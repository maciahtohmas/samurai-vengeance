Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 December 2026

Samurai Vengeance is operated by Billfiti Studios, a one-person studio in San Diego, California. This policy explains what the game stores, why, and how to get it back or get rid of it.

What we collect

Everyone: when you visit, the game sends anonymous counts to our own server, such as "a visitor arrived", "the tutorial was finished" or "stage 3 was cleared", plus a rough source like "came from a search engine". These are added to daily totals. They contain no name, no email, no IP address we keep, no cookies and no device ID. To tell new visitors from returning ones, the game checks progress already saved in your browser and sends only "new" or "returning". If the game hits an error, it also sends the error message, where in the game it happened and what you were doing (for example "playing stage 12"), so we can fix it. Our server notes only the general type of device and browser, such as "iPhone, Safari", and stores nothing that identifies you.

If you play as a guest: your progress is kept in your browser's local storage and we never see it. If you send feedback, we receive your message, any email you choose to give, and your browser type, so we can reply and reproduce bugs.

If you accept the consent bar: Google Analytics (through Firebase) records how the game is played, such as stages reached and where runs end, and may set cookies to do so. Google AdSense may also load and set its own cookies to serve and measure ads.

If you create an account: email address, display name, gameplay statistics (stage reached, kills, playtime, coins earned, combo scores) and purchase status. Your display name, best stage, combo and kills appear on the leaderboard, which other signed-in players can see. If you join through a friend's invite link, we record that the invite was used.

Our host, Netlify, keeps standard request logs (including IP addresses) for a limited time to run and protect the site.

We do not collect or store payment card information at any point. See Payments below.

How we use it

  • To save your progress and sync it across devices
  • To show the leaderboard
  • To see which parts of the game work and where players get stuck
  • To reply to feedback and fix bugs

We do not sell or share your data for money, ever.

Legal basis (EU, EEA and UK)

Account data is processed to provide the service you signed up for. Analytics and advertising run only with your consent, which you can withdraw by clearing this site's data in your browser and choosing Decline. The anonymous visit counter relies on our legitimate interest in knowing whether the game works, and stores nothing on your device.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout pages. Your card details are entered on Stripe's domain and are never transmitted to or stored by us. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, along with the email address you gave Stripe, so the purchase can be attached to your account. Stripe keeps its own payment records as the law requires, even if you delete your account.

Advertising

The game may display advertising to players without a purchase. Ads load only after you accept the consent bar. Advertising partners may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure and serve ads. Any purchase removes advertising entirely.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage for your consent choice, settings and game progress. We set no cookies of our own. If you accept the consent bar, Google Analytics and Google AdSense may set cookies.

Do Not Track

The game does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals. Instead, analytics and advertising never run unless you accept the consent bar.

Your rights

You may request an export of your data, correction of anything inaccurate, or deletion of your account and everything attached to it, at any time. In the game, Settings → Export My Data downloads your data and Profile → Delete Account deletes your account, save data and leaderboard entry. To also remove feedback you sent or invite records, email us. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Data retention

Account data is retained while the account is active. When you delete your account, your account, save data and leaderboard entry are removed straight away, and anything else we hold about you is removed within 30 days of an email request. Anonymous daily counts contain nothing about you and are kept.

Children

Accounts are not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU and EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Contact

Questions, export requests or deletion requests: contact@samuraivengeance.com
