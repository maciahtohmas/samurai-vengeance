Ki and the special

Ki is the blue bar under your health. Fill it and you can wipe the screen with one press, or spend part of it to parry when your parry is on cooldown. This page covers how the bar fills, what the special does, and when to use it. I took every number from the game's code.

The keys

InputKi special
KeyboardX, when the bar is full
TouchThe Ki button, which only appears on the right-hand pad once the bar is full

Press X early and you will see KI NOT READY. Nothing is spent, so there is no harm in checking.

How the bar fills

The bar holds 100 Ki. It only fills when your sword connects. A swing that hits nothing adds nothing, so spamming attack at empty air will not charge it.

  • Every hit that lands: 1 Ki, plus 3 for each level of Spirit, the Ki upgrade in the Dojo.
  • A Finisher that kills: an extra 15 Ki. The Finisher is the third hit of the combo, and a parry turns your next swing into one (see the Parry Guide).

The hits-to-fill column ignores Finisher kills, so in a real fight the bar fills faster than that. Your Ki carries over from one stage to the next during a run, and resets to empty when you start a new run. Premium players start every stage with at least 30.

What the special does

Press it with a full bar and every enemy on the screen takes 500 damage plus 80 for each level of Strength, all at once, wherever they are. Then the screen freezes for 75 frames, about 1.25 seconds, with a blue flash. The whole bar is spent.

A normal enemy on Warrior has 20 health plus 5 per stage, so even at Strength 1 the special kills every normal enemy on screen up to stage 112. On Demon (35 plus 8 per stage) it clears the screen up to stage 68 at Strength 1. Further up, it leaves them badly hurt instead of dead.

Two things to know before you press it:

  • It breaks your combo chain. Your next swing starts again from the first hit, and an open PUNISH window is cancelled.
  • You cannot act during the freeze either. It is a breather, not a free attack window.

The Ki Parry

The bar has a second use. If you press parry while it is on cooldown and you have at least 30 Ki, and an enemy or projectile is close, the game spends 30 Ki and parries anyway. You will see KI PARRY. If nothing is near, the press is ignored and your Ki is untouched. The full rules are in the Parry Guide.

That makes every Ki decision a trade. 30 Ki is almost a third of a special. Spend it to survive a hit you would otherwise take, or bank it for the screen wipe.

When to use it

Crowded stages

The special is at its best when the screen is full. Swarm stages (more enemies, less health each) and Storm of Arrows stages (everything shoots) are where it saves the most damage. Using it on one grunt wastes most of it.

Bosses

A stage boss has 900 health plus 25 per stage, so the Iron Oni at stage 10 has 1,150. A special at Strength 1 takes off 580, which is just over half. That matters because a boss enters Phase II at half health: it moves 1.6 times faster and fires a fan of three shots instead of one. Two ways to play it:

  • Push it early: hit the boss down a little, then use the special to jump it most of the way through Phase I in one press.
  • Save it for Phase II: keep the full bar for when the boss speeds up, and use the 1.25 second freeze to reset your footing.

I prefer saving it. Phase II is where runs end. The Boss Guide covers each boss.

Glass Blade stages

Glass Blade doubles the damage you take. Clearing the screen before anything reaches you is worth more there than anywhere else.

What to buy first

For most players: Iron Guard first, for a wider parry window. Then Spirit to level 2 or 3, which cuts the hits to fill the bar from 25 to 15 or 10. Strength helps the special too, but each level only adds 80 to it, while Spirit lets you use it far more often.

Common questions

How do you use the Ki special in Samurai Vengeance?

Fill the Ki bar to 100, then press X on a keyboard or the Ki button that appears on the touch pad. It hits every enemy on screen at once.

How do you fill the Ki bar faster?

Land hits. Each hit adds 4 Ki at Spirit level 1 and 3 more per Spirit level, and a Finisher that kills an enemy adds 15 more. Swings that miss add nothing.

How much damage does the Ki special do?

500 damage to every enemy on screen, plus 80 for each level of Strength. At Strength 1 that is 580, which kills any normal enemy on Warrior up to stage 112.

Does Ki carry over between stages?

Yes. Your Ki stays with you from stage to stage during a run and resets when you start a new run.

What is KI NOT READY?

It appears when you press the special before the bar is full. Nothing is spent; keep landing hits.
