Contact

Samurai Vengeance is made by one person at Billfiti Studios. Every message goes straight to the developer.

Email

contact@samuraivengeance.com

That's the best way to reach me about anything: a bug, an idea, a question about your account, press, or a business enquiry.

Reporting a bug

The quickest route is inside the game. Open Settings from the menu and tap Report next to Feedback / Bug Report. If you'd rather email, these details make a bug much faster to find:

  • The device and browser you were playing on, for example an iPhone 15 in Safari or a Chromebook in Chrome.
  • The stage number and difficulty.
  • What you did just before it went wrong, and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot or short screen recording, if you can grab one.
