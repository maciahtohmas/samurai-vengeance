Best free samurai games you can play in your browser

A short list of samurai and ninja games that are free and run in a browser tab, with no download and no install.

Upfront: I made one of the games on this list. My name is Maciah Thomas, I am a solo developer in Southern California, and Samurai Vengeance is my first game. I have tried to describe it the same way as the others, and it is not at the top.

How I picked these

When I started building a samurai game I went looking for what already existed in the browser. A lot of what turns up in search is a dead Flash page, a mobile app with a web landing page, or a demo that wants a download. Every game below was checked to have a live page where it plays for free in a browser at the time of writing. I have only described what each game's own page says it is. I have not included ratings or player counts, because those change and I cannot check them for you.

Most of these are hosted on Poki or CrazyGames. Both are free game portals that run ads, which is how the games stay free. The links go to each game's page on those portals.

The list

Samurai Legacy

An action game with light RPG elements. You play Nobunaga, a former samurai fighting his way through catacombs to stop a shadow lord. It is built in Unity, runs in a desktop browser, and has a block on the right mouse button, which makes the sword fighting more deliberate than most browser games.

Play Samurai Legacy on CrazyGames

Samurai Sam

A ragdoll action game built around a grappling hook. You press and hold to aim, release to fire, and swing into enemies, so momentum does a lot of the cutting for you. New weapons unlock as you go, including a katana and a naginata. It plays on desktop and on phones.

Play Samurai Sam on Poki

Final Ninja

A stealth platformer from Nitrome. You play Takeshi, a ninja trying to leave his old life while his former boss hunts him, across 20 levels with a rope hook, throwing stars and shadows to hide in. It started life as a Flash game and now runs in HTML5. There is also a prequel, Final Ninja Zero, on the same site.

Play Final Ninja on Poki

Samurai Madness

A roguelite action game where you fight waves of enemies with a katana. Instead of pressing an attack button, you drag to draw the path your samurai cuts along. It runs in desktop and mobile browsers.

Play Samurai Madness on CrazyGames

Rabbit Samurai

A grappling-hook puzzle platformer where the samurai is a rabbit. One click or tap fires the hook, and each level is about working out the right swing. It is the gentlest game on this list and the one I would hand to a younger player. A sequel, Rabbit Samurai 2, is on the same site.

Play Rabbit Samurai on Poki

Samurai Showdown

A sword-fighting game set in ancient Japan, where rogue samurai threaten a kingdom. You move with WASD, attack with the mouse to build combos, and enemies drop in slow motion when you cut them down. It also has an arena survival mode with waves of samurai.

Play Samurai Showdown on CrazyGames

Samurai Vengeance

Mine. A 2D side-on action platformer with a three-hit sword combo, a parry that sets up a double-damage counter, a dash and a Ki special. There are 1,000 stages, with a boss every ten. It runs in the browser on a phone or a computer, with keyboard or touch controls, and progress saves in your browser without an account.

Play Samurai Vengeance

Which one to start with

  • You want sword duels with blocking: Samurai Legacy.
  • You want something quick on a phone: Samurai Sam or Samurai Madness.
  • You want stealth and platforming: Final Ninja.
  • You want puzzles more than fighting: Rabbit Samurai.
  • You want a parry-focused platformer with bosses: that is the one I built.

A note on portals

Portal pages carry ads around the game, and some games show an ad between levels. That is normal and is what pays the developers. If a game here stops loading or moves, let me know through the contact page and I will update or remove it.

If you do try Samurai Vengeance, the beginner's guide covers the first ten stages and the parry guide explains the one skill that matters most.
