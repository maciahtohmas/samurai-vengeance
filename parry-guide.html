How to parry

The parry is the most useful thing you can learn in Samurai Vengeance. This page covers the keys, the exact timing, what a parry does to each enemy, and the Ki Parry. I took every number here from the game's code.

The keys

InputParry
KeyboardQ or E
TouchThe shield button (🛡) on the right-hand pad

Older builds put the parry on S. S is now the standard WASD down key, so holding S (or the down arrow) drops you through a platform instead. If your fingers still reach for S, that is why nothing happens.

One press gives you one parry. The window opens the moment the key goes down and runs for a fixed time. Holding the key does not keep it open, and you have to let go and press again before the next parry can start. The help screen says "hold", and holding is fine, but think of it as a timed press.

The parry window

The game runs at a fixed 60 updates per second. The parry window is 7 frames plus 3 for every level of Iron Guard, the parry upgrade in the Dojo.

Iron GuardWindowCost to reach
Level 110 frames, about 0.17 sFree
Level 213 frames, about 0.22 s100 coins
Level 316 frames, about 0.27 s150 coins
Level 419 frames, about 0.32 s220 coins
Level 522 frames, about 0.37 s300 coins

That is 770 coins from level 1 to level 5, and the window more than doubles. You are also invincible for the whole window plus 4 extra frames, so even a parry that catches nothing still protects you for a moment.

After you press, there is a cooldown of 52 frames, a little under 0.9 seconds, before a normal parry can fire again. Pressing during the cooldown does nothing unless you can pay for a Ki Parry (see below).

You do not have to wait for contact

This is the part most people miss. While the window is open, the game parries anything inside a reach around you, not just things touching you:

  • Arrows and boss shots within 240 game pixels that are flying toward you. Every one in range is destroyed in the same frame, so you can clear a whole volley with one press.
  • Enemies within 118 pixels on roughly your own level. Only one enemy is parried per frame, so a crowd will not all bounce at once.

Something already flying away from you does not count. Neither does an enemy on the platform above or below you.

On a phone, the shield button lights up gold when something parryable is in that reach and your parry is ready. It goes dim while you are on cooldown without 30 Ki. If you press when it lights, you will not be early.

What a successful parry gives you

Every parry shows a blue PARRY!, freezes the screen for 6 frames (about a tenth of a second) so you can see it land, and does two more things.

Combo

A parry adds to your combo counter and resets its timer, the same as landing a hit. Your damage rises 6% per combo point, up to 2.8 times at a combo of 30, so parries build damage without you swinging.

ParriedCombo added
Arrow or boss shot+3
Grunt, archer or shield-bearer+3
Tengu dive+4
Boss body+5

PUNISH

A yellow PUNISH! appears and you get 50 frames, about 0.8 seconds, where your next swing skips straight to the Finisher. The Finisher is the third hit of the combo and does 2 times normal damage. Normally you need to land two hits first to reach it. Swing straight after the parry or the window is gone. Using your Ki special also cancels it.

Parry and Ki

A parry does not put Ki in the bar by itself. Ki comes from hits: 4 per hit at Spirit level 1, plus 3 more per Spirit level, and a Finisher that kills something adds an extra 15. Because PUNISH turns your next swing into a Finisher, a parry followed by a kill is one of the fastest ways to fill the bar.

The Ki Parry

If you press parry during the cooldown with at least 30 Ki, and something is actually close (an enemy within 130 pixels or a projectile within 170), the game spends 30 Ki, skips the cooldown and parries anyway. You will see KI PARRY in light blue. If nothing is near, the press is ignored and your Ki is untouched, so you cannot drain the bar by accident between fights.

I use it when two things hit me back to back, like an archer's arrow right after a grunt. It is a real trade: 30 Ki is almost a third of your special.

What a parry does to each enemy

Grunts and archers

The enemy is turned to face away and its body cannot hurt you again for 115 frames, almost 2 seconds. An archer can still shoot during that time, because its bow runs on its own reload timer. Grunts are the easiest parry in the game, so they are the best practice.

Arrows

The arrow is destroyed. On Warrior an arrow does about 7 damage, so parrying it is less about health and more about the free PUNISH and combo. Dashing through an arrow also works, but gives you nothing back.

Shield-bearers

A shield-bearer blocks any hit from the side its shield faces. A parry breaks its guard for 120 frames, 2 full seconds, and turns it away from you. The shield drops and goes dim while it is broken. Normally it takes the shield-bearer about 0.6 seconds to turn round, so the parry is the safest way to get two seconds of free hits from the front.

Tengu

Tengu only hurt you during a dive. Parry the dive and it is cancelled, and that tengu waits at least 130 frames before it can dive again. The dive hits for about 11 on Warrior, so this one is worth learning.

Bosses

Parry a boss as it walks into you and it bounces off and turns around, you are pushed back a step, and it cannot touch you again for at least a second. You take nothing instead of the 26 or so a body hit costs on Warrior, and you get +5 combo and a PUNISH. Boss shots parry the same way as arrows. In Phase II a boss fires a fan of three, and one well timed press clears all three if they are in reach together. The Boss Guide has the full fight.

How to practise

  1. Replay the tutorial from the main menu. Step 4 fires slow practice arrows that do no damage, and it tells you if you were too early or too late.
  2. Play the first few stages, which are grunts only, and parry every grunt before you swing.
  3. Parry, then swing once. Get that rhythm until it is automatic.
  4. Buy Iron Guard before Strength. A wider window helps every fight; extra damage only helps the ones you are already winning.
