Beginner's guide: your first 10 stages

Everything I would tell a friend before their first run: the controls, which difficulty to pick, what shows up in stages 1 to 9, what to buy, and how to beat the Iron Oni on stage 10. Every number comes from the game's code.

If you played an older build, note that parry moved from S to Q and E. S now drops you through a platform.

The tutorial

Your first stage runs a six-step tutorial: move, double jump, defeat an enemy, parry an arrow, dash, then clear the stage. During the first two steps the enemies are frozen, so you cannot get hit while finding the buttons. The parry step fires slow practice arrows that cannot hurt you and tells you whether you pressed too early or too late. You can skip it at any time, and replay it later from the Tutorial button on the main menu.

You start with 100 HP, and your first swing does 52. Peace is genuinely forgiving and a fine way to learn the controls. Warrior is the one I balance the game around. Demon is for after you can parry without thinking. You can switch difficulty later and carry on from the furthest stage you have reached.

What you will fight in stages 1 to 9

Each stage has 3 enemies plus one more for every third stage, so stage 1 has 3 and stage 9 has 6. Clear them all to finish the stage.

  • Stages 1 to 3: grunts only. They walk their platform and hurt you by walking into you. They only connect when you are both standing on the same level, so jumping over one is always safe.
  • From stage 4: archers, about 15% of enemies. They shoot every 1.5 to 2.5 seconds if you are close enough. Parry the arrow or dash through it.
  • From stage 5: spikes on some platforms. Standing on them costs about 14 HP every third of a second. Stage 5 can also have one elite enemy with a red glow, 2.2 times the health and 30% faster.
  • From stage 6: tengu, about one enemy in seven. They hover and dive at you from above. They only hurt you during the dive.
  • Stage 8: the first stage modifier, Swarm. More enemies, but each has just over half the usual health.

Shield-bearers do not appear until stage 11. The Enemy Guide covers every type in more detail.

Your sword and the combo

Attack three times in a row for a three-hit chain: an overhead, a side cut that does 1.2 times damage, and a Finisher that does 2 times. Every hit you land raises the combo counter, and each point adds 6% damage, up to 2.8 times. The counter drops if you go a little over two seconds without landing something.

Parry

Press Q or E just before something reaches you. A parry blocks the hit, turns the enemy away and gives you a PUNISH, which makes your next swing a Finisher. At the start the window is only about 0.17 seconds. The parry guide covers the timing, the reach and the Ki Parry in full.

Ki and the special

The bar at the top fills by 4 Ki per hit you land (more with the Spirit upgrade), and a Finisher that kills something adds an extra 15. At 100 it is full and X, or the burst button on touch, hits every enemy on screen for 580 damage at Strength level 1 and freezes time for a little over a second. Ki carries over from stage to stage until you spend it, which matters a lot for stage 10. The special also resets your three-hit chain.

Dash

The dash throws you forward and makes you invincible for its length plus a few frames. It goes through arrows, enemies and spikes. The cooldown is 0.7 seconds. If you are about to take a hit and the parry is on cooldown, dash through it.

The Dojo

Coins from each stage go to the Dojo on the main menu. Each upgrade goes up to level 5.

My advice for a first run: Iron Guard first, then Vitality. There is also a Health Potion for 80 coins that restores half your HP, which can save a boss attempt.

Between stages you heal 30% of your max HP, so you do not need to finish every stage at full health.

Revive

From stage 6 on, dying can offer a revive. It puts you back where you fell with half your HP, 2.5 seconds of invincibility, and every projectile on screen cleared. Depending on your build it costs a short ad or a five-second wait. It is not offered on your first death of a session, and not on the death straight after a revive.

Stage 10: the Iron Oni

Every tenth stage is a boss. Stage 10 is the Iron Oni, Warden of the First Gate, with 1,150 HP. The arena is flat ground and it always rains. No modifier ever lands on a boss stage.

What it does

  • Walks back and forth across the arena.
  • Fires a single shot at you about once every 1.1 seconds.
  • Hits hard on contact: about 26 on Warrior, so four touches end your run from full health.
  • Below half health (575 HP) it enters Phase II: 60% faster, shooting about every 0.6 seconds, and each volley is a fan of three faster shots.

How to beat it

  1. Bring a full Ki bar. Fill it on stage 9 and do not spend it. Your special does 580 at Strength 1, and the boss enters Phase II with at most 574 HP, so one special ends the fight the moment Phase II starts.
  2. Jump over it. The body only hurts you when you are both on the ground at the same height. Jump as it walks toward you, land behind it and hit its back.
  3. Parry the shots. Each parried shot gives you a PUNISH. Swing straight away for a double-damage Finisher.
  4. In Phase II, stop stepping sideways. The fan covers the space you would dodge into. Parry it or dash through it, or just use the special.
