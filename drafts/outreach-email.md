# Outreach templates

Fill in the [brackets] for each person. Mention one specific video or post of theirs, and only if you have actually watched or read it. Send to one person at a time, not as a group email.

Hi [name],

I watched your [video/post] on [specific game or topic], and [one honest sentence about what you liked or noticed]. That is why I am writing to you in particular.

Play it here: https://samuraivengeance.com

You are free to stream it and post videos of it, including monetized videos. You do not need to ask first. A link in the description is appreciated but not required.

Screenshots, key art, a fact sheet and descriptions are in the press kit: https://samuraivengeance.com/press-kit

If you do cover it, I would like to see it. And if you find a bug, I want to hear about that too.

Thanks for your time,
Maciah Thomas
