# The Indigo Ronin, Issue Three: The Twin

Approved by Maciah, Nov 21 2026. Season 1 (free).

Story: Aizumi's twin brother was separated at birth and raised at the Crimson Keep by the Blood Shogun as a weapon. He does not know Aizumi exists. The Shogun sends him after Lord Sakae's surviving people wearing Sakae's indigo armor. The issue ends when both are unmasked and see the same face (the in-game boss is the Shadow Twin, stage 20).

## Characters (paste the matching line into every prompt)

- AIZUMI: Japanese samurai, about thirty, black hair in a topknot, a thin scar across his brow, stubble, indigo lacquered armor with GOLD lacing and a gold sash, katana.
- THE TWIN: identical build and face to Aizumi but no scar and younger in the eyes, indigo lacquered armor with CRIMSON lacing and a crimson sash, a black horned helmet and a black demon-face mask (menpo) covering his face until page 9.
- THE OLD WOMAN: elderly farmer from Issue 2, grey hair in a bun, worn brown kimono.
- THE BLOOD SHOGUN: huge figure in deep red armor with a crested helmet, seen from a distance.

## How to make each page

Attach two reference images every time: Issue 2 page 12 (the figure in the road, for style and palette) and Issue 1 page 3 (Aizumi's face). Then paste the page prompt. Ask for portrait format.

Style line for every prompt:
"Use the attached comic pages as the style and character reference: same hand-painted comic look, dark ink linework, the same Aizumi. Portrait format. No text, no speech bubbles, no captions, no border."

## Pages

1. The road below the Crimson Keep at dusk. THE TWIN stands alone in the middle of a muddy road, masked, hand on his sword, blood-red rice paddies on both sides and the Keep on a hill behind him. Seen from behind Aizumi's shoulder.
   Caption: He wore the indigo of Lord Sakae's house, down to the knot of the cords. Aizumi had buried every man who had the right to wear it.

2. Close-up of THE TWIN, chest up: the black demon mask, horned helmet, indigo armor with crimson lacing. Red dusk light from one side.
   Caption: "Whose armor is that," Aizumi said. / "The Shogun gives it to the ones he sends after Sakae's people," the man said. "So they know who has come for them."

3. Flashback in muted sepia-red: a boy of about eight alone in a large stone courtyard at the Crimson Keep, practising with a wooden sword. High above on a balcony, THE BLOOD SHOGUN watches, small and dark against the sky.
   Caption: He had been raised at the Keep. Nobody there had ever told him where he came from, and he had never thought to ask. A sword does not ask who forged it.

4. AIZUMI and THE TWIN draw their katanas at the same moment, facing each other across the road, ten paces apart, low angle, dust rising.
   Caption: They drew together. Aizumi had stood across from fast men before.

5. Action: AIZUMI strikes; THE TWIN parries at the last instant, blades meeting, a burst of sparks between them.
   Caption: The man waited for the swing. Nobody waited for the swing. Aizumi had spent a whole winter learning to.

6. Wide shot: both in the exact same sword stance, mirror images on either side of the frame, a red sunset between them reflected in the flooded rice paddies.
   Caption: Every stance Aizumi took, the other man had already taken. It was like fighting the water in the paddies.

7. THE OLD WOMAN and a few farmers in straw hats stand at the edge of the paddies, watching the duel in the distance, tense.
   Caption: The farmers stopped working to watch. None of them had seen anyone last this long against him.

8. Close combat: a shallow cut opens on AIZUMI's forearm, while his blade slices the cord that holds THE TWIN's demon mask.
   Caption: He drew first blood. Aizumi took the cord of his mask.

9. Both strike at the same instant: THE TWIN's demon mask and horned helmet fly off one way, AIZUMI's helmet the other. Motion lines, debris.
   Caption: They struck at the same moment, the way men do when they have been taught by the same hands. They never had been.

10. Both men stagger back a few steps, breathing hard, heads down, faces turned away from the viewer, swords still raised.
    Caption: Both men stepped back to breathe. Neither of them looked up right away.

11. Close-up of THE TWIN's face, unmasked for the first time: exactly Aizumi's face, but younger in the eyes and with no scar on the brow, shocked, red light on one side.
    Caption: It was his own face. No scar. No winter in it.

12. Split panel, left and right halves: AIZUMI (scar) and THE TWIN (no scar), face to face in profile, identical, lit by the last red light, swords between them.
    Caption: Thirty years apart, and the same face. Neither of them lowered his sword. / The Indigo Ronin returns in Issue Four.
