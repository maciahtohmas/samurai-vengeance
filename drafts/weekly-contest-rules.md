## 1. Sponsor

Billfiti Studios, [legal name or business entity], [mailing address],
contact@samuraivengeance.com ("Sponsor").

This contest is a game of skill. The winner is decided by performance in the
game, not by chance.

## 2. Contest period

Each contest week starts Monday at 00:00 UTC and ends the following Monday at
00:00 UTC. The first contest week begins [date]. Sponsor may run the contest
for as many weeks as it chooses and will announce the last week in advance at
samuraivengeance.com.

## 3. Who can enter

Open to anyone, anywhere the contest is not prohibited by law, who has a free
Samurai Vengeance account. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's
permission before their display name is used in a social media shoutout. Not
open to Sponsor, its family members, or anyone who helped build the game.

## 4. How to enter

1. Go to samuraivengeance.com and create a free account (or sign in).
2. Play the game while signed in. Your best stage, best combo and kills for the
   week are recorded automatically and shown on the leaderboard under This Week.

There is no other way to enter and no limit on how many times you play. Only
one account per person. Entering by any automated means is not allowed.

## 5. How the winner is chosen

At the end of each contest week, the eligible player with the highest Best
Stage on the This Week leaderboard wins. If players are tied, the higher Best
Combo for the week wins. If they are still tied, the higher Kills for the week
wins. If they are still tied, each tied player receives the prize.

Sponsor will review the winning account before awarding the prize. Sponsor
may disqualify any score it reasonably believes was gained by cheating,
modifying the game, exploiting a bug, or using another person's account, and
award the prize to the next eligible player.

## 6. Prize

Each week's champion receives:

- the Champion sword style in the game, kept for good;
- a crown next to their name on the leaderboard until the next champion is
  crowned;
- a place on the Hall of Champions page at samuraivengeance.com/champions;
- a shoutout with their display name on Billfiti Studios' TikTok, if they
  agree to it.

Once a month, Sponsor may also name a stage in the game after the best weekly
champion of that month. The name stays on the stage for as long as Sponsor
runs the game.

Prizes are in-game items and recognition only. They have no cash value, cannot
be exchanged for money, and cannot be transferred to another account.

## 7. Notifying the winner

The champion sees a message in the game the next time they sign in, and the
crown and sword style appear on their account. Sponsor will email the winner
at the address on their account to ask whether they want a TikTok shoutout.
No reply means no shoutout; the other prizes still apply.

The display name of each champion is shown on the leaderboard and the Hall of
Champions page. Nothing else about the winner is published without their
permission.

## 8. Privacy

Information collected for the contest (account email and display name, and
the details a winner gives to claim a prize) is used only to run the contest
and deliver the prize, as described at samuraivengeance.com/privacy.

## 9. General conditions

Sponsor may cancel, change or pause the contest if a technical problem,
cheating, or anything else outside its control affects the fair running of the
contest. Sponsor is not responsible for lost connections, devices, or entries
that fail to record.

This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or run by TikTok, Facebook, Reddit,
itch.io, Google, Apple or any other platform, and they are not responsible
for it.
