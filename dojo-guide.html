Dojo upgrades: what to buy first

The Dojo is where your coins go. There are five upgrades, each with five levels, and a health potion. This page lists exactly what every level does and costs, and the order I would buy them in. I took every number from the game's code.

Where to find it

Open the main menu and tap 🏯 Dojo. Your coin total is at the top. You start every upgrade at level 1, so each one takes four purchases to reach level 5.

The cooldown stays at 42 frames, about 0.7 seconds, at every level. A longer dash helps you cross gaps and get through arrow volleys, but you get nothing back for it, where a parry gives you combo and a PUNISH.

Health Potion

80 coins restores half your maximum health at once. It only works when you are hurt, and you can use it during a stage. Save it for a boss fight you are close to winning.

What to buy first

New players

  1. Iron Guard to level 2 (100)
  2. Spirit to level 2 (120)
  3. Vitality to level 2 (100)
  4. Iron Guard to level 3 (150)
  5. Then whatever you die to most

That is 470 coins for a wider parry, a special every 15 hits and 20 more health. One Daily Boss win early on pays for most of it.

Stuck on a boss

Vitality and Iron Guard. Boss fights are lost to a few big hits, and both of these are about surviving them. Bring a potion.

Playing on Demon

Max Iron Guard before anything else. Enemies hit hard enough on Demon that a parry you can land reliably matters more than any amount of damage.

Leave for later

Strength and Swiftness. They help, but only in fights you are already winning.

Common questions

What should I upgrade first in Samurai Vengeance?

Iron Guard. A wider parry window helps in every fight, and it is the cheapest way to take less damage. Then Vitality or Spirit, depending on whether you are dying or running out of Ki.

How much does it cost to max every upgrade?

4,070 coins in total. Each upgrade has five levels and you start at level 1, so it is four purchases each.

What does Strength do?

It raises your base slash damage by 12 per level, from 52 at level 1 to 100 at level 5, and adds 80 per level to your Ki special.

Is the Health Potion worth it?

It costs 80 coins and restores half your maximum health at once. It is worth it in a boss fight you are close to winning, not on a normal stage.

Why did the Dojo show different prices before?

An old price formula was overwriting the real prices on the buttons. That is fixed in the December update, and the prices on this page are the ones the game charges.
