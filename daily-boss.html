The Daily Boss

Once a day there is a boss fight waiting on the main menu, worth more coins than most stages put together. This page covers how it works, how strong it gets, what it pays, and how to beat it. I took every number from the game's code.

Where to find it

Open the main menu and look for the ☠ Daily Boss button. It is there until you beat the boss that day. Win, and the button disappears until tomorrow. Lose, and it stays, so you can try again as many times as you like.

The day turns over at midnight on your own device's clock.

What you get

Beating the Daily Boss pays 500 coins, plus 50 for every 10 stages you have reached. It also counts toward your bosses-slain achievements.

How strong it is

The Daily Boss grows with you. Its health is 300 plus 5 for every stage you have reached, then raised 10% for each level of Strength you own, then doubled. Buying more damage does not make it a pushover; it keeps the fight about the same length.

It fights like a stage boss. It walks the floor toward you, and hits hard if it reaches you: about 26 on Warrior. It fires an aimed shot about every 1.1 seconds. At half health it enters Phase II, moves 1.6 times faster, and fires a fan of three shots about every 0.6 seconds instead.

The fight is a single arena with flat ground and no platforms, so there is nowhere to hide and nothing to climb. Your health starts full.

How to beat it

  1. Parry the body, not just the shots. When it walks into you, a parry bounces it back, keeps you from taking about 26 damage, adds 5 to your combo and gives you a PUNISH, so your next swing is a double-damage Finisher. Swing straight away. The Parry Guide has the timing.
  2. In Phase II, parry the fan. One well timed press clears all three shots if they are in reach together. Dashing through them also works, but gives you nothing back.
  3. Arrive with a full Ki bar if you can. The Daily Boss does not reset your Ki, so any Ki you still had when you went back to the menu comes with you (after reopening the game, the bar starts empty). A special takes 580 or more off in one press. On an early Daily Boss that is most of its health. See the Ki Guide.
  4. Do not chase. Let it come to you. Most damage in this fight comes from running into the boss while trying to land one more hit.
  5. Lost? Upgrade, then come back. The button stays all day. Iron Guard (parry window) and Vitality (more health) help more here than Strength, because Strength also raises the boss's health.

Want to practise on a fixed boss first? The stage 10 Iron Oni has the same moves. The Boss Guide covers every stage boss.

Common questions

What is the Daily Boss in Samurai Vengeance?

A one-off boss fight you can take once a day from the main menu. Beat it for a big coin reward. The button disappears after you win and comes back the next day.

When does the Daily Boss reset?

At midnight on your device's clock. The menu button shows again once the date changes.

How many coins does the Daily Boss give?

500 coins, plus 50 for every 10 stages you have reached. A player who has reached stage 100 gets 1,000.

Can I try the Daily Boss again if I lose?

Yes. The Daily Boss button stays on the menu until you beat it that day, so you can try as many times as you like.

Why is my Daily Boss harder than my friend's?

Its health grows with the highest stage you have reached and with your Strength level, so it stays a real fight as you get stronger.
