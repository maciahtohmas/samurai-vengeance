⚔ Enemies
GruntPatrols and attacks at close range. Easy to parry.
ArcherFires arrows from a distance. Dash through them.
ShieldBlocks frontal attacks. Hit from behind or use Ki.
BossEvery 10 stages. Two phases — stay aggressive.
⚡ Mechanics
ComboHit enemies without taking damage. Higher combo = more coins.
Ki GaugeFills when hitting enemies. Full bar = Ki Special attack.
ParryPerfect timing reflects damage. Grants brief invincibility.
DashInvincible during dash. Goes through arrows.
SpikesAvoid or jump over. Damage on contact.
CoinsSpend in Dojo Shop to upgrade HP, damage, speed.
💡 Pro Tips
• Parry just before an attack lands for best results
• Dash through archer arrows to close distance safely
• Hit Shield enemies from behind — or use Ki Special
• Daily Challenges give bonus coins — check every day
• Boss fights every 10 stages — save your Ki for them
• Buy HP upgrades first — survival beats damage early on
